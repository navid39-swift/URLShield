URLShield checks every link for you – before you tap it – and it never opens it.

CHECK BEFORE YOU TAP
Paste a message, an email or a link. In about a second URLShield finds every link, shows you the real website behind it and gives you a clear verdict:
• Likely phishing – don't open it
• Suspicious – be careful
• Known site – the official domain of a well-known website
• Hidden destination – a shortened link that hides where it goes
• Open platform – a page anyone could have published

IT TELLS YOU WHY
No mysterious scores. URLShield explains the warning signs it found:
• look-alike addresses such as paypa1.com or amaz0n.it
• brand names placed inside a fake domain
• links to a bare IP address
• the hidden "@" trick that disguises the real destination
• shortened links that hide their destination

AN AI THAT LIVES ON YOUR IPHONE
The detection model was trained on more than 800,000 real and phishing web addresses and runs entirely on your device. It works offline, it's instant, and nothing you paste, share or receive ever leaves your iPhone.

STOP SCAM TEXTS AUTOMATICALLY
Turn on the URLShield SMS filter in Settings: messages from unknown senders that contain a likely phishing link go straight to Junk. It runs offline, and URLShield never stores or sends your messages.

CHECK FROM ANY APP
Select text in Messages, Mail or Safari, tap Share and choose URLShield – the verdict appears without leaving the app.

SPOT THE FAKE
A quick game that trains your eye to recognize the tricks scammers use every day. Great for parents, grandparents and anyone who has ever doubted a link.

PRIVACY FIRST – FOR REAL
• No account
• No tracking, no ads, no analytics
• No data collected – "Data Not Collected" on the App Store
The only thing URLShield downloads is a digitally signed update of its detection model, verified on your iPhone before it is used.

HONEST BY DESIGN
No tool can guarantee that a link is safe – hacked websites and brand-new tricks can look normal. URLShield never says "safe": it gives you a clear, explained second opinion. When in doubt, contact the company through its official app or website.
